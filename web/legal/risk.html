中文

你需要知道的五件事

  1. 任何人（包括 LEGAVIK）都无法找回、重置或绕过你的恢复密码（Recovery Password）。 一旦遗失，对应的恢复地图（Recovery Map）将无法读取。
  2. 三样东西一起妥善保存： 每个恢复地图版本对应的恢复钥匙（Recovery Kit）、存储凭证（Storage Receipt）和恢复密码。
  3. 现在就下载独立恢复工具，与恢复材料放在一起。这样即使不登录 LEGAVIK 账户、不依赖我们的服务器，也能恢复。
  4. 你的加密恢复地图会永久发布到公共存储网络 Arweave。 即使关闭账户，也无法删除、撤回或修改。
  5. LEGAVIK 保存你的恢复信息，但不保证任何底层账户或资产一定能够进入或恢复。

1. 你的恢复材料

恢复一份恢复地图，需要匹配的恢复钥匙、存储凭证和恢复密码。这些材料由你自己保管。请保存安全的备份，不要改动恢复文件，并把恢复密码与恢复钥匙分开存放。避免通过普通邮件、聊天工具或共用设备传送恢复材料。

2. 恢复密码与登录密码

账户登录密码用于登录 LEGAVIK 账户，可以重置。恢复密码不同：它由你创建和保管，用于保护加密恢复地图的密钥，LEGAVIK 从不接收。LEGAVIK 没有第二把钥匙、备份密钥或管理员密钥。遗失的恢复密码任何人都无法找回。

3. 版本必须匹配

恢复材料只对应一个已完成的恢复地图版本。请勿混用不同恢复地图或不同版本的材料。新版本不会把旧材料变成新版本的材料。

4. 材料遗失、新版本与旧版本

LEGAVIK 无法补发遗失的恢复钥匙或恢复密码。如遗失，你可以在账户中为恢复地图创建新版本；新版本会有新的恢复钥匙、存储凭证和恢复密码，第一个版本之后的每个新版本消耗一次更新次数。旧版本可能在账户中显示为已替换，但 LEGAVIK 无法作废旧材料：持有匹配的旧恢复钥匙、存储凭证和旧恢复密码的人，仍可能读取旧版本。如不希望旧材料继续可用，请安全销毁你掌握的副本。LEGAVIK 无法销毁他人持有的副本。

5. 尽早验证恢复

保存好材料后，请用独立恢复工具完成一次初始自助恢复验证。如你的方案包含恢复演练，演练记录不包含任何秘密或恢复内容，只包含标识信息（例如账户、版本和交易编号）、时间、完整性哈希和通过/未通过结果，绝不包含你的恢复密码、恢复钥匙内容或明文。

6. 秘密：安全规则

请遵守服务条款第 4 条的安全规则：不要以可读取的形式保存单独即可控制账户或资产的秘密，也绝不要发给客服；应改为记录其保存位置。LEGAVIK 员工和客服工具（包括 AI 客服）绝不会向你索要这些秘密。客服聊天设计为自动删除检测到的部分秘密，但这只是最后防线，消息可能已经到达客服服务商。

7. 加密与非托管

恢复地图内容在你的浏览器中加密后才保存，LEGAVIK 无法读取。LEGAVIK 不持有你的底层资产，不控制你的账户或钱包，也不会因你使用本服务而取得转移你资产的任何权利。

8. 永久公开存储

发布前，你会被要求确认加密恢复地图将永久发布到 Arweave。LEGAVIK 使用自己的钱包签名并支付发布费用，你无需 Arweave 钱包。公众可以看到加密文件和技术元数据，但看不到明文。已发布的文件无法删除、撤回或修改。关闭账户只会删除账户与该文件之间的关联，不会删除文件本身。

9. 恢复结果与及时更新

持有正确的材料可以读取恢复地图。之后能否进入底层账户或资产，取决于 LEGAVIK 无法控制的因素，包括你的信息是否及时更新、第三方规则、设备、相关人员和合法权限。当邮箱、手机号、两步验证、设备、账户找回方式，或恢复地图中提到的人员和地点等重要情况发生变化时，请复核并更新恢复地图。LEGAVIK 无法自动得知这些变化。

10. 法律权属

恢复地图或持有恢复材料，不认定任何人对账户或资产的所有权、权益或法律权限，也不能替代遗嘱、授权书等正式法律安排。如其他人日后使用匹配的材料，LEGAVIK 不判断其法律权利。

11. 设备与账户安全

请保护好你的电脑和手机、浏览器配置、邮箱、两步验证和备份。如设备或账户可能已被入侵，请先处理安全问题，再用于恢复。

12. 第三方基础设施

本服务和独立恢复依赖浏览器、互联网、邮件、支付、公共存储网络及网关，以及你自己的设备。LEGAVIK 不经营这些设施，无法保证它们永不变化或故障。如 LEGAVIK 停止运营，适用服务条款第 11 条（至少提前 90 天通知，并公开发布独立恢复工具）。

13. 你的法定权利

本告知说明恢复如何运作以及各方的责任，并非概括性免责声明。本告知不影响澳洲消费者法项下依法不得排除的保障或救济，其中的风险分配仅在法律允许的范围内适用。

LEGAVIK Recovery & Security Notice Version 2.5 / 恢复与安全告知 第 2.5 版

Version 2.5, effective 10 December 2026. Read together with the Terms of Service Version 2.7 and Privacy Policy Version 1.9.

English

The five things to know

  1. No one, including LEGAVIK, can retrieve, reset or bypass your Recovery Password. If you lose it, the matching Recovery Map cannot be read.
  2. Keep all three together and safe: the Recovery Kit, the Storage Receipt and the Recovery Password for each Recovery Map version.
  3. Download the Independent Recovery Tool now and keep it with your materials, so you can recover without your LEGAVIK account or our servers.
  4. Your encrypted Recovery Map is published permanently to Arweave, a public storage network. It cannot be deleted, recalled or changed, even if you close your account.
  5. LEGAVIK preserves your recovery information. It does not guarantee that any underlying account or asset can be accessed or recovered.

1. Your recovery materials

Recovering a Recovery Map requires the matching Recovery Kit, Storage Receipt and Recovery Password. You hold these materials yourself. Keep secure backup copies, do not alter the recovery files, and keep the Recovery Password separate from the Recovery Kit. Avoid sending recovery materials through ordinary email, messaging or shared devices.

2. Recovery Password and login password

Your account login password gives access to your LEGAVIK account and can be reset. Your Recovery Password is different: you create and keep it, it protects the key that encrypts your Recovery Map, and LEGAVIK never receives it. LEGAVIK holds no second key, backup key or administrator key. A lost Recovery Password cannot be recovered by anyone.

3. Versions must match

Recovery materials belong to one completed Recovery Map version. Do not mix materials from different Recovery Maps or versions. A new version does not convert old materials into materials for the new version.

4. Lost materials, new versions and old versions

LEGAVIK cannot reissue a lost Recovery Kit or Recovery Password. If you lose them, you can create a new version of your Recovery Map in your account; it comes with a new Recovery Kit, Storage Receipt and Recovery Password, and each version after the first uses one update entitlement. An older version may be marked as replaced in your account, but LEGAVIK cannot revoke old materials: anyone holding the matching old Recovery Kit, Storage Receipt and old Recovery Password may still be able to read that old version. If you do not want old materials to remain usable, securely destroy the copies under your control. LEGAVIK cannot destroy copies held by others.

5. Verify your recovery early

After saving your materials, complete an initial self-recovery verification using the Independent Recovery Tool. Where your plan includes a recovery drill, the Drill Record contains no secrets or recovery content. It holds identifiers (such as account, version and transaction references), the time, integrity hashes and the pass/fail result, and never your Recovery Password, Recovery Kit contents or plaintext.

6. Secrets: the security rule

Follow the security rule in clause 4 of the Terms of Service: do not store a secret that by itself controls an account or asset in readable form, and never send one to support. Record where it is kept instead. LEGAVIK staff and support tools, including AI support, will never ask for these secrets. Our support chat is designed to automatically remove some secrets it detects, but this is a backstop only: the message may already have reached the support provider.

7. Encryption and non-custody

Recovery Map content is encrypted in your browser before storage. LEGAVIK cannot read it. LEGAVIK does not hold your underlying assets, control your accounts or wallets, or obtain any right to move your assets because you use the Service.

8. Permanent public storage

Before publication you will be asked to confirm that the encrypted Recovery Map will be published permanently to Arweave. LEGAVIK signs and pays for publication with its own wallet; you do not need an Arweave wallet. Public observers can see the encrypted file and technical metadata, but not your plaintext. Published files cannot be deleted, recalled or changed. Closing your account removes the link between your account and the file, not the file itself.

9. Recovery outcomes and keeping information current

Holding the correct materials lets the Recovery Map be read. Whether an underlying account or asset can then be accessed depends on things outside LEGAVIK's control, including whether your information is current, third-party rules, devices, people and legal authority. Review and update your Recovery Map when important things change, such as email, phone, two-step verification, devices, account recovery methods, or the people and places named in it. LEGAVIK cannot know about these changes automatically.

10. Legal ownership

A Recovery Map, or possession of recovery materials, does not establish anyone's ownership of, entitlement to or legal authority over an account or asset. It does not replace formal legal arrangements such as a will or power of attorney. If another person later uses matching materials, LEGAVIK does not decide their legal rights.

11. Device and account security

Secure your computers and phones, browser profiles, email, two-step verification and backups. If a device or account may be compromised, fix that first before relying on it for recovery.

12. Third-party infrastructure

The Service and independent recovery rely on browsers, the internet, email, payment, the public storage network and gateways, and your own devices. LEGAVIK does not operate these and cannot promise they will never change or fail. If LEGAVIK ceases operating, clause 11 of the Terms of Service applies (at least 90 days' notice and public release of the Independent Recovery Tool).

13. Your legal rights

This Notice explains how recovery works and who is responsible for what. It is not a blanket disclaimer. It does not affect guarantees or remedies that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law, and its risk allocations apply only to the extent permitted by law.
