LEGAVIK Product Schedule Version 2.7 / 产品附表 第 2.7 版

第 2.7 版，自 2026 年 12 月 10 日起生效。本附表构成服务条款第 2.7 版的一部分。具体权益和收费以客户接受的订单和结账页为准，并受适用法律约束。所有价格均以澳元（AUD）计价，适用时已包含 GST。

1. Essential — first year / 基础版首年

中文 澳元 840。包含一份完整恢复地图、客户端加密、加密长期保存、恢复钥匙和存储凭证、自助恢复及初始验证、现行指南和模板、标准客户支持，以及首个服务年度内一次恢复地图内容更新。

2. Standard — first year / 标准版首年

中文 澳元 1,680。包含基础版全部内容，另加：首个服务年度内两次恢复地图内容更新（基础版为一次）；一次自助恢复演练并留下演练记录；关键账户恢复线索整理。客户支持为标准客户支持。

3. Custom / 定制方案

中文 澳元 7,000 起。定制恢复与交接规划服务。定制方案须先与我们沟通后开通，其服务范围、交付物和更新次数（首年至少两次）仅以签署的订单或定制附表为准。本方案不使 LEGAVIK 托管客户资产或取得处置客户资产的任何权限；任何交接功能仅在明确上线后适用。

4. Upgrade and extra update / 升级及额外更新

中文 基础版升级标准版：澳元 1,176，不按已过时间比例减价。包含次数用完后的额外更新：澳元 280。额外更新仅在当前服务年度内有效，过期未用不折现。自助复核（Recovery Review）不限次数；复核本身不消耗更新次数，完成内容更新才消耗。

5. Annual Maintenance / 年度维护

中文 基础版：每 12 个月澳元 336，含一次更新、每六个月自检提醒、指南和模板以及标准客户支持。标准版：每 12 个月澳元 672，含两次更新、一次自助恢复演练及演练记录、指南和模板以及标准客户支持。定制方案：每 12 个月澳元 1,680 起，以订单为准。如提供按月分期，基础版每月澳元 28、标准版每月澳元 56，均为支付 12 个月年费的方式。

6. Billing currency, tax and activation / 币种、税费与激活

中文 先付款；付款成功后套餐激活，首个服务年度自激活时开始。所有费用均以澳元计价，适用时包含 GST。订单和结账页显示应付总额。依法需要时，税务发票列明 LEGAVIK PTY LTD、ABN 和 GST。

7. Renewal and annual obligations / 续费与年度义务

中文 自动续费绝不预先勾选，须客户主动选择并留存记录。续费前至少 30 天，我们会告知客户下一年度、费用、分期安排、续费状态以及关闭续费的方法。客户可以在下一年度开始前关闭续费。年度开始后，单纯改变主意并不当然免除年度费用，但不影响任何法定救济。

8. Refunds and duplicate payments / 退款与重复付款

中文 改变主意：付款后 14 天内，且该次付款后尚未上传恢复地图的，客户可以申请全额退款。上传后已经产生永久存储成本，因此一般不提供单纯因改变主意的退款。经核实的重复付款予以退款。所有法定消费者救济均予保留，每一项退款申请均由人工审查。

9. Payment failure and purchased rights / 扣款失败与已购权益

中文 分期扣款失败后，LEGAVIK 可以通知客户、重试扣款、要求更新付款方式，并在法律允许的范围内按比例暂停未付费的未来维护服务。不续费维护或关闭账户，不会删除已完成的加密档案，也不会使匹配的客户自持恢复材料失效。

Version 2.7 · 2026-12-10

LEGAVIK Product Schedule Version 2.7 / 产品附表 第 2.7 版

Version 2.7, effective 10 December 2026. This Schedule forms part of the Terms of Service Version 2.7. Actual entitlements and charges are those shown in the accepted Order and at Checkout, subject to applicable law. All prices are in Australian dollars (AUD) and include GST where applicable.

1. Essential — first year / 基础版首年

EN AUD 840. Includes one complete Recovery Map, client-side encryption, encrypted long-term storage, the Recovery Kit and Storage Receipt, self-recovery with initial verification, current guides and templates, standard customer support, and one Recovery Map content update in the first service year.

2. Standard — first year / 标准版首年

EN AUD 1,680. Includes everything in Essential, plus: two Recovery Map content updates in the first service year (instead of one); a self-guided recovery drill with a Drill Record; and organising recovery clues for key accounts. Support is standard customer support.

3. Custom / 定制方案

EN From AUD 7,000. A tailored recovery and handover planning service. The Custom plan starts after a conversation with us, and its scope, deliverables and number of updates (at least two in the first year) are only those stated in a signed Order or Custom Schedule. It does not give LEGAVIK custody of, or any authority to deal with, a customer's assets, and any handover feature applies only once expressly launched.

4. Upgrade and extra update / 升级及额外更新

EN Essential-to-Standard upgrade: AUD 1,176, not pro-rated by time elapsed. An additional update after included entitlements are used: AUD 280. Extra updates expire unused at the end of the current service year and have no cash value. Self-service Recovery Reviews are unlimited; a Review does not use an update entitlement, but a completed content update does.

5. Annual Maintenance / 年度维护

EN Essential: AUD 336 per 12 months, including one update, a six-monthly self-check reminder, guides and templates, and standard customer support. Standard: AUD 672 per 12 months, including two updates, a self-guided recovery drill with Drill Record, guides and templates, and standard customer support. Custom: from AUD 1,680 per 12 months, as stated in the Order. If offered, monthly instalments are AUD 28 (Essential) or AUD 56 (Standard) per month, as a way of paying the 12-month fee.

6. Billing currency, tax and activation / 币种、税费与激活

EN Pay first; the plan activates after successful payment, and the first service year starts on activation. All charges are in AUD and include GST where applicable. The Order and Checkout show the total payable. Where required, a tax invoice identifies LEGAVIK PTY LTD, its ABN and GST.

7. Renewal and annual obligations / 续费与年度义务

EN Auto-renewal is never preselected and requires the customer's active, recorded choice. At least 30 days before renewal, we notify the customer of the next annual period, the amount, any instalments, the renewal status and how to switch renewal off. Renewal can be switched off before the next term begins. An ordinary change of mind after an annual term starts does not by itself cancel the annual fee, without affecting any legal remedies.

8. Refunds and duplicate payments / 退款与重复付款

EN Change of mind: a customer may request a full refund within 14 days after payment if no Recovery Map has been uploaded since that payment. After an upload, permanent storage costs have been incurred, so a refund for change of mind alone is not ordinarily available. Confirmed duplicate payments are refunded. All statutory consumer remedies remain available, and each refund request is reviewed by a person.

9. Payment failure and purchased rights / 扣款失败与已购权益

EN After a failed instalment, LEGAVIK may notify the customer, retry, ask for a valid payment method and, subject to law, proportionately suspend unpaid future maintenance services. Not renewing maintenance or closing an account does not delete completed encrypted archives or invalidate matching customer-held recovery materials.
